Why theatre?

Some of the people who have made the biggest difference in my life have been theatre teachers. They helped me see how much a classroom can matter.

I want students to understand that theatre is more than costumes, performances, or learning lines. It can build confidence, public-speaking skills, teamwork, and the courage to step outside a comfort zone.

Most importantly, I want students to feel seen and know they have a place to belong.
